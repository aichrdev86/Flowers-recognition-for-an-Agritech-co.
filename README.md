# Flower Recognition for an AgriTech Company

**GreenTech Solutions Ltd.**, a cutting-edge company in the agricultural technology sector, is facing the critical need to implement an advanced automatic flower recognition system within its daily operations. The ability to automatically identify flowers is crucial for several strategic and operational reasons:

* **Optimization of Agricultural Procedures:** Automatic flower recognition allows for the automation and acceleration of key agricultural processes, such as assessing plant health, managing crop yields, and scheduling maintenance activities.
* **Plant Health Monitoring:** The capacity to quickly and accurately identify flowers enables GreenTech Solutions to monitor crop health in real-time, detecting early signs of vegetative stress, diseases, or nutrient deficiencies.
* **Data-Driven Decisions:** Automatic flower recognition provides detailed and reliable data that supports informed agronomic decisions, including the optimization of phytosanitary treatments, irrigation management, and agricultural resource allocation.
* **Operational Efficiency and Cost Reduction:** Automating the flower identification process reduces dependence on human labor for repetitive tasks, allowing GreenTech Solutions to allocate resources more efficiently and reduce overall operational costs.

## Project Benefits

Implementing an advanced Computer Vision model for automatic flower recognition will bring numerous tangible advantages:

* **Increased Productivity:** Reduction in time spent on manual flower identification, freeing up resources for more strategic, high-value-added activities.
* **Quality Improvement:** Guaranteeing a more precise and uniform classification of flowers, thereby improving the overall quality of the agricultural services and products offered.
* **Technological Innovation:** Demonstrating leadership in the adoption of cutting-edge technologies, positioning GreenTech Solutions as a pioneer in digital and sustainable agriculture.

## Project Details

GreenTech Solutions Ltd. has launched a strategic initiative to develop a prototype AI-based Computer Vision model for automatic flower recognition. The provided dataset includes two main categories of flowers, split as follows:

| Flower Category | Train Images | Validation Images | Test Images |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Daisy** | 529 | 163 | 77 |
| **Dandelion** | 746 | 201 | 105 |

### Project Objective

The primary objective is to develop a highly accurate and robust model capable of automatically classifying flowers, achieving the best possible **F1-score (macro)** on the test dataset. To optimize model performance, the use of advanced techniques such as **transfer learning** and **data augmentations** using PyTorch's timm library is strongly recommended.

### Recommended Approach

Students are required to configure and train a deep neural network using **PyTorch**, providing detailed documentation of the methodologies adopted and the decisions made throughout the development process. It is essential to carefully evaluate the model's advantages and challenges, including its ability to adapt to variations in environmental conditions and produce consistent, reliable results in real-world scenarios.

Integrating this innovative solution will allow GreenTech Solutions Ltd. to further consolidate its position in the agricultural market, offering advanced solutions that improve operational efficiency, promote sustainability, and meet the growing needs of customers in the modern agricultural sector.
